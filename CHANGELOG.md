# Changelog

## 1.4.0 (2026-11-04)

First public release.

- Copies KOReader's reading statistics when a device connects: automatically, after asking, or only on request.
- Year view: a heat map of reading time, or each book's colour, overlapping on days with several books.
- Month view: a calendar with a bar for each book and the time read each day.
- Filter by book, and click a day to see what you read, for how long, and how many pages.
- Detects records written while a device clock was wrong, and offers to correct them in KOReader's own database, after saving a backup. Nothing changes unless you choose it.
- Settings for syncing, heat map colour and scale, opening view, week start, when a new day starts, and hiding books you only opened briefly.
- Imports a statistics file by hand, and merges statistics from more than one device.
